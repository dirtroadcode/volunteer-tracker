volunteer tracker Dirtroad Organizing

No volunteers left behind.

A free, open source volunteer tracker using Google Sheets with some Apps Script magic. Copy it, run Set Up once, and every volunteer gets a first contact and a follow-up reminder on your own calendar.

Copy the tracker View the Sample
  1. 1

    Copy the tracker

    Use the button above or open the Sample and choose File → Make a copy. The copy lands in your Google Drive with all five tabs and the automation attached.

  2. 2

    Run Set Up once

    In your copy, open Volunteer Tools → Set Up. It will create a sign-up form for you. The form just needs to ask for Name, Email, and Phone. Google then shows a one-time permission screen. It may say “Google hasn’t verified this app” and name your own email address as the developer — that’s expected, because the copy’s script belongs to you, not to us. Click Advanced → Go to “…” (unsafe), or just Continue on newer screens, then allow the permissions. It only lets your own copy add reminders to your own calendar.

  3. 3

    Work from your calendar

    New signups land in your Directory and put a first-contact reminder on your calendar. When you're ready to give someone work, choose Volunteer Tools → New Action — pick the volunteer and an Ask, date the deadline, and the reminders take care of themselves.

Good to know

Menu of Asks
The things your volunteers can do — canvassing, phone banking, whatever your campaign needs. Rename the examples right on the tab; no code, just typing.
Settings
Four timing numbers you can retune any time: first contact the day after signup, a nudge 2 days before a deadline, a thank-you 3 days after, all at 9 am.
