# Coordinator-only boundary

The tool's only job is reminding the coordinator: calendar events for first
contact, pre-touch, and post-touch. It never messages volunteers — no
MailApp/GmailApp anywhere. Appreciation is deliberately the coordinator's
call, made concrete as reminders, and the "specific ask" is always spoken by
a human, never templated by the script. We rejected the obvious "just email
the volunteer" automation because volunteer-facing behavior at copy scale
can't be vetted or opted into reliably, and the PRD's non-goals are explicit
that this product is about reminding the coordinator, not outreach.
